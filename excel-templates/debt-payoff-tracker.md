## The Problem This Template Solves

Over 60% of Americans experience debt, and most of them don't know where to start. With multiple credit cards, loans, and lines of credit, it is easy to pay minimums on everything and feel stuck — while interest quietly eats your progress.

The **Debt Payoff Tracker Excel Template** gives you a single, structured view of every debt you carry, lets you model your payoff strategy, and tracks your progress automatically so you stay motivated and on target.

---

## Key Features

- **Full Debt Inventory** — log every creditor, balance, interest rate, and minimum payment in one place.
- **Payoff Strategy Planner** — model both the **Debt Snowball** (smallest balance first) and **Debt Avalanche** (highest interest first) methods.
- **Interest Savings Calculator** — see exactly how much interest you save by choosing the right payoff order.
- **Progress Tracking** — monitor payments, remaining balances, and your estimated debt-free date.
- **Goal Tracking** — set a target payoff date and track milestones along the way.
