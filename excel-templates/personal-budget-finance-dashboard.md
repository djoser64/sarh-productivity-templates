## The Problem This Template Solves

A good budget is useless if you cannot stick to it. Most people track expenses inconsistently, forget recurring costs, or have no clear picture of where their money actually goes. Without a structured dashboard, small leaks sink bigger savings goals.

The **Personal Budget & Finance Dashboard Excel Template** turns raw transactions into clear insights you can act on — all inside Microsoft Excel.

- **Income vs. Expense Tracking** — log income and expenses and compare against your budget in real time.
- **Budget vs. Actual Analysis** — see where you overspent and where you saved.
- **Net Worth Tracking** — monitor assets and liabilities over time.
- **Savings Goals** — set and track progress toward emergency funds and financial goals.
- **Visual Charts** — dynamic charts for spending trends and category breakdowns.
