## How to Import the Blueprint into Make.com
1. Log in to your **Make.com** account and open **Scenarios**.
2. Click **Create a new scenario**.
3. At the bottom toolbar, click the **three dots (`...`)** > **Import Blueprint**.
4. Select `MarketPulse_Make_Lite_Blueprint.json` and click **Save**.
5. Connect your **Notion** account and configure your API/webhook modules before running a test.
